Sunday 11 Oct · about 1 hour

Sunday prep

Everything here is vegan. Do it before Sunday dinner, which uses half the broccoli straight away.

Roasted chickpeas

Used Mon and Fri

4 cans cooked chickpeas (390 g) · 3 tbsp oil · salt · pepper

Rinse, pat very dry and roast at 175°C for about 45 min, stirring now and then. Keep half in the fridge for Monday and freeze half for Friday's tacos.

Roasted broccoli

Used Sun and Mon

3 heads broccoli (250 g each) · 2 tbsp olive oil · salt · pepper

Cut into wedges and roast at 225°C for about 15 min.

Tomato sauce

Used Mon and Sat

4 shallots · 3 garlic cloves · 2 pinches chilli flakes · 2 tbsp olive oil · ½ dl tomato paste · 2 tsp sugar · 3 cans whole tomatoes (390 g) · 2 vegetable stock cubes

Soften onion, garlic and chilli in the oil. Fry in the paste and sugar for a minute. Add tomatoes and stock cubes, simmer 30 min, then blend smooth. Split into 2 jars: one for the fridge, one for the freezer.

Green herb sauce

Used Sun, Tue and Thu

1 pot parsley · 1 bunch dill · zest and juice of 1 lemon · 1 garlic clove · 1½ dl olive oil · salt

Blend or chop very finely. Store in a jar with a thin layer of oil on top.

Pickled red onion

A topping every night you cook

1 red onion · 1 dl apple cider vinegar · 1 tsp sugar · ½ tsp salt

Slice thinly, pour over the mixed brine and cover. Keeps about 2 weeks in the fridge.

Order of work

  1. Oven to 175°C. Rinse, dry and season the chickpeas; roast 45 min.
  2. Chop all onions and garlic in one go (sauce, pickle, green sauce).
  3. Start the tomato sauce.
  4. Pickle the red onion. Blend the green sauce.
  5. Chickpeas out, oven up to 225°C, roast the broccoli 15 min.
  6. Blend the tomato sauce and split it into 2 jars. Split the chickpeas in half.
  7. Cool everything quickly, then store as below. Start Sunday dinner.

Sunday 11 Oct

Crispy potatoes with roasted broccoli, sausage and herby ricotta

25 min after prepReplaces ICA's gnocchi
Roasted broccoli · ½ Green herb sauce Pickled onion Spinach

Everyone

  • ½ the roasted broccoli
  • Green sauce to drizzle
  • Pickled onion
  • 1 kg small potatoes
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 2 handfuls spinach
  • Salt, pepper

You

  • 1 pack plant-based sausage (about 200 g)
  • White-bean ricotta: 1 can white beans, 2 tbsp green sauce, salt

Family

  • 1–2 packs mini chorizo (240 g)
  • 250 g ricotta mixed with 2 tbsp green sauce
  1. Boil the potatoes about 15 min until just tender. Drain, halve and let them steam dry.
  2. Mash or blend the white beans with green sauce and salt. Stir green sauce into the ricotta.
  3. Fry your sliced sausage in a little oil and set aside. In the same pan, fry the potatoes until golden, about 10 min, and spoon out your share. Add the sliced chorizo to the rest and fry 3 min.
  4. Toss the potatoes with spinach to wilt. Warm the broccoli 5 min in the oven.
  5. Spread ricotta on the plates (beans for you), then add potatoes, sausage and broccoli. Drizzle with green sauce and finish with pickled onion.

Monday 12 Oct

Chana masala with broccoli, rice and raita

20 minVegan for everyone except the extras
Tomato sauce · jar 1 Roasted chickpeas · ½ Roasted broccoli · ½ Pickled onion Spinach Coriander Raita, again Fri

Everyone

  • 1 jar tomato sauce
  • The fridge half of the chickpeas
  • ½ the roasted broccoli
  • Pickled onion
  • 4 portions rice
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 1½ tbsp garam masala, 2 pinches cinnamon, 2 tsp turmeric
  • 2 tbsp oil, 2½ dl water
  • 2 handfuls spinach, ½ pot coriander
  • Raita base: ½ cucumber, 1 small garlic clove, salt

You

  • 1 dl oat yoghurt for your raita
  • Dairy-free flatbread

Family

  • 3 dl yoghurt for their raita
  • 4 naan
  1. Oven to 200°C. Cook the rice.
  2. Fry ginger and spices in the oil. Lower the heat, add the tomato sauce and water and simmer 5 min. Add the chickpeas and spinach and heat until the spinach wilts.
  3. Grate the cucumber, squeeze out the water and mix with grated garlic and salt. Split it between the two yoghurts.
  4. Warm the broccoli and bread in the oven for a few minutes.
  5. Top with coriander and pickled onion. Serve the raita on the side.

Tuesday 13 Oct

Baked fish with potatoes and green-sauce crème fraîche

You're out · family only, 3 portions30 min
Green herb sauce Pickled onion

Family

  • 500 g cod or other white fish
  • 800 g potatoes
  • 1 tbsp butter, ½ lemon
  • 2 dl crème fraîche + 3 tbsp green sauce
  • 3 dl frozen peas
  • Pickled onion on the side
  • Salt, pepper
  1. Oven to 200°C. Boil the potatoes, 15–20 min.
  2. Put the fish in a greased dish, season, add a few lemon slices and dots of butter. Bake 15–20 min.
  3. Stir the green sauce into the crème fraîche.
  4. Boil the peas 3 min. Serve with pickled onion on the side.

Thursday 15 Oct

Pasta with broccoli, green sauce and crispy chickpeas

20 min
Green herb sauce Spinach Family grated cheese

Everyone

  • About 1 dl green sauce
  • 4 portions dried pasta (penne works well for kids)
  • 2 heads fresh broccoli
  • 1 can chickpeas
  • 2 garlic cloves, a pinch of chilli flakes
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 handfuls spinach
  • Salt, pepper

You

  • Garlic crumbs: 1 dl panko, 1 tbsp olive oil, 1 grated garlic clove

Family

  • Grated cheese (the shared bag)
  1. Put on the pasta water. Cut the broccoli into small florets.
  2. Toast the panko with oil and garlic in a dry pan, about 3 min.
  3. Fry the drained chickpeas in the oil until crisp, 5–7 min. Add sliced garlic and chilli for the last minute.
  4. Cook the pasta, adding the broccoli for the last 3 min. Save 1 dl pasta water, then drain.
  5. Toss with the chickpeas, spinach, green sauce and a splash of pasta water until the spinach wilts.
  6. Top with crumbs or cheese.

Friday 16 Oct

Chickpea tacos

20 minBuild your own
Roasted chickpeas · frozen ½ Pickled onion Raita, as Monday Coriander Peppers & mushrooms Family grated cheese

Everyone

  • The frozen half of the chickpeas (to the fridge Thursday night)
  • Pickled onion
  • Taco spice: 1 tbsp oil, 2 tsp cumin, 2 tsp smoked paprika, 1 tsp oregano, ½ tsp chilli flakes, 2 tbsp tomato paste, a splash of water
  • 8–12 soft tortillas
  • 1 red pepper and ½ the mushrooms, 1 tbsp oil
  • 2 avocados, ½ lime
  • The other ½ cucumber, 250 g cherry tomatoes, ½ head iceberg lettuce
  • 1 can sweetcorn, 1 jar salsa, ½ pot coriander

You

  • Raita with 1 dl oat yoghurt

Family

  • Raita with 3 dl yoghurt
  • Grated cheese (the shared bag)
  • Optional: 400 g beef mince fried with 1 tsp each cumin and smoked paprika
  1. Fry the chickpeas in the oil with the spices, 3–4 min, until crisp again. Stir in the tomato paste and a splash of water until sticky and coated. Lightly crush a few so they stay put in the tortilla.
  2. Slice the pepper and mushrooms and fry them hot, 5–6 min, fajita style. Salt.
  3. If making mince, brown it in a separate pan.
  4. Mix the raita (a little grated cucumber, garlic and salt into each yoghurt). Mash the avocados with lime and salt. Chop the rest of the vegetables. Warm the tortillas.
  5. Set everything out with coriander and pickled onion and let everyone build their own.

Saturday 17 Oct

Pizza night

25 minStore-bought dough
Tomato sauce · jar 2 Pickled onion Peppers & mushrooms Family grated cheese

Everyone

  • 1 jar tomato sauce (to the fridge Friday night)
  • Pickled onion
  • 2 rolls ready-made pizza dough (check it's vegan)
  • The other ½ of the mushrooms, 1 red pepper
  • Olives, rocket

You

  • Plant-based grated cheese (optional) or extra olive oil

Family

  • Grated cheese (the shared bag)
  • 100–150 g ham or salami
  1. Heat the oven as the dough pack says, usually 225–250°C. Slice the vegetables.
  2. Unroll the dough and cut off a piece for your own pizza. Spread the sauce and add toppings.
  3. Bake your pizza on its own tray, 10–12 min. Top with rocket.
